Data points to a changing economy. By 2035, the field of quantum computing is expected to generate $1.3 trillion to $2.7 trillion in global economic impact. Health care roles like nurse practitioners — recently named the “hottest” job in health care by The Wall Street Journal — and speech-language pathologists are projected to grow at several times the national average through 2034 and beyond.
