Phoenix was changing rapidly when sociologist Albert Mayer arrived at ASU in 1968.New homes and neighborhoods were spreading across the desert as the metropolitan area's population soared. Mayer made the region his living laboratory, studying urban demography and Phoenix’s expansion while hundreds of ASU students packed into his popular course The Modern City.
