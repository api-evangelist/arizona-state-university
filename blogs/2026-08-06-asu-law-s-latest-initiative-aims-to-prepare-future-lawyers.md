Arizona State University is at the forefront of AI education, and that goes for all disciplines.According to a 2024 American Bar Association survey, ASU’s Sandra Day O’Connor College of Law was one of only 20% of law schools that were actively teaching or training students in artificial intelligence and its legal implications.
