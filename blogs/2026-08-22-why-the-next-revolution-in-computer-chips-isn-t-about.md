From smartphones and electric vehicles to medical devices and artificial intelligence, nearly every aspect of modern life depends on semiconductors. But after decades of making computer chips smaller and more powerful, the industry is reaching practical limits.The next leap forward may come from a different approach known as advanced packaging — combining multiple specialized little chips, or “chiplets,” into a single package that delivers greater performance while using less space and energy.
