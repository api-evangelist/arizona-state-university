For generations, choosing a college came before taking a college class. Today, more learners are getting started earlier, earning college credit before submitting an application or stepping onto a campus.That shift is driving record growth for Arizona State University's Universal Learner Courses, or ULCs. More than 43,000 learners participated in ULCs this year — an increase of more than 40% from last year.
