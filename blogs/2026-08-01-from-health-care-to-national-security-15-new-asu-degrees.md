With the addition of 15 new degree programs this fall, The College of Liberal Arts and Sciences at Arizona State University is meeting growing workforce demands in industries ranging from health care to national security. In addition, this August, the inaugural cohort of the McKenna Life Sciences, Business and Entrepreneurship Program will start their Sun Devil journey in a new degree pathway that advances a new generation of medical and biotechnology innovations.
