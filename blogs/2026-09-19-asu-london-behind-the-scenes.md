The opening of ASU London, an independent, U.K.-degree-awarding higher education provider founded by Arizona State University, kicks off this week — and our reporter Scott Bordow is on the ground reporting on the launch.At the new institution in the heart of the city, at the Clerkenwell campus of City St. George’s, University of London, students can complete a three-year bachelor's degree and then pursue an accelerated one-year master's degree at ASU.
