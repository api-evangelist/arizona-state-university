An Arizona State University professor is helping to tell a more complete story of art in America.Earlier this year, the Phoenix Art Museum received a historic donation of 185 Native artworks, the single largest gift of Native art in the museum’s history. The modern and contemporary works, by 99 artists representing 44 tribal nations, were donated by The William P. Healey Collection of Native American Art.
