As the school year gets underway for families, there’s a hidden danger made possible by an otherwise innocuous piece of equipment in many home offices: 3D-printed weapons. A recent monthslong investigation by The Beam revealed cases across the state where K–12 students brought such weapons — including switchblades, box cutters and butterfly knives — into school.
