A university delegation from Taiwan was able to see Arizona State University’s innovation in action, far beyond the two institutions’ shared work in the semiconductor field.Earlier this month, a delegation from National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University visited ASU and agreed to explore opportunities for faculty and student exchanges, as well as areas of potential collaboration.
