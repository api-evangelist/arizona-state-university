It's already understood that long-term opioid use and withdrawal both lead to changes the brain, some of which scientists are still mapping out.Now, research from the Arizona State University Department of Psychology and Biodesign Institute has shown that these changes extend beyond just neurons, affecting the brain’s network of support cells.
