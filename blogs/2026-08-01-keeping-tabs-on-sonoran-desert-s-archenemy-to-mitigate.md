It has been called the archenemy of the Sonoran Desert: Buffelgrass, which is native to Africa, Asia and the Middle East, was brought to arid areas of the United States in the 1930s to be planted for cattle forage and erosion control.Today, the invasive grass species is the most likely to cause significant wildfire damage. But it's not the only one.
