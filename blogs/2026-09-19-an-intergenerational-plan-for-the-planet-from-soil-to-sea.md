Laura Turner Seydel used to think cows were the problem.Growing up in a family devoted to restoring bison and protecting wildlands, she had seen the damage overgrazing could do. Then a film by ASU Professor of Practice Peter Byck about soil health changed the way she thought about agriculture, climate and one of the most overlooked tools for planetary repair.
