Halloween is coming to Arizona State University’s West Valley campus a bit early this year, in the form of treats for moms and expecting mothers. On Sept. 26, the Maricopa County Department of Public Health — in conjunction with ASU — will hold its biannual WIC (women, infants and children) Spooktacular Fall Festival community event.
