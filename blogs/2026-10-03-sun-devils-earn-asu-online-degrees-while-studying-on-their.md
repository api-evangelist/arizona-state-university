Harlen Doucette is following in his siblings’ footsteps by shaping his Arizona State University journey to fit his life — studying on a tour bus while performing with his family and taking a break when he needs one.Doucette, who is pursuing a degree in industrial and organizational psychology through ASU Online, is part of The Doucettes, a family of bluegrass gospel singers based in Texas.
