Every spring, Major League Baseball transforms metro Phoenix.Fans in team jerseys stream into 10 ballparks, restaurant patios fill up and hotels welcome visitors escaping colder parts of the country. But the Cactus League is more than a cherished Arizona tradition: It is a powerful economic engine, and the 2027 season could be in jeopardy.
