When young defendants accept plea deals in adult criminal court, do they fully understand the rights they are giving up? That question is at the center of a New York case that has drawn the attention of Arizona State University psychologist Melanie Fessinger, whose research examines how people make decisions in the legal system. In this case, a teenager accepted a plea agreement that included a lengthy prison sentence and a waiver of his right to appeal his case later.
