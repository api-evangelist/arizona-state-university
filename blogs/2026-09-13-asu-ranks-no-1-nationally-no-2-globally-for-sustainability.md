Arizona State University has been ranked No. 1 in the United States and No. 2 in the world for sustainability in the 2026 Sustainable Campus Index, earning its fourth consecutive Platinum rating through the Sustainability Tracking, Assessment and Rating System, or STARS — ahead of Cornell University, Stanford University and the University of California, Berkeley.
