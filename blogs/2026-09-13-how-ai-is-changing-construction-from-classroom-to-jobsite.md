As summer temperatures continue to soar in the Valley of the Sun, construction workers are among the most vulnerable to heat-related illness. But picture a construction site where artificial intelligence analyzes data from wearable biosensors and environmental monitors worn by construction workers, looking for early signs of fatigue and heat stress; and when it detects them, it sends personalized, real-time alerts — before symptoms start.
