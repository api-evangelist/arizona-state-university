Story by May BuschMany professionals assume influence belongs to the people with the biggest titles.But over the course of my career, I’ve found the opposite is often true. Some of the most influential people at work are not managers at all. They are the people others trust, seek out and want to collaborate with.That’s because influence has less to do with your title than most people think.
