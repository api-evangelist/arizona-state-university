As her Scottsdale Chaparral High School classmates slept on the bus, exhausted from the long flight, Shelby Connell stared in awe at the sights of London.It was mid-June, and Connell was on her senior trip. But as the bus carrying the students lurched through London’s streets, Connell’s thoughts were not on the high school experience she was leaving behind but on her future — as one of the first students to attend ASU London.
