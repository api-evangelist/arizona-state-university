For many Arizonans, water has become both a daily concern and a difficult puzzle. Headlines about Colorado River shortages, groundwater limits and long-term supply questions have made the issue feel urgent, but not always easier to understand.The reality is that Arizona’s water story changes from place to place, depending on local water portfolios, groundwater conditions, regulations and planning requirements.
