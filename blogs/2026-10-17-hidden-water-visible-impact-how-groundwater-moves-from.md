When rain falls in Hawai‘i and soaks into the subsurface, some of it drains to streams. But some of it leaks below streams and coastal communities to enter the ocean as submarine groundwater discharge. Until recently, researchers weren’t sure of how that hidden water moves from Hawai‘i’s mountains to its coastlines.
